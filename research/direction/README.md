The direction uses **certified boundary intervals and shared arithmetic**, comparing forward deletion masks with a relative-rank table. Both can build exact maps in one forward pass. Their physical tradeoff is unmeasured. The prototype supplies mathematical/software evidence, not hardware timing or established novelty. Primary literature links include reading limits. No market data were successfully downloaded or numerically evaluated during these passes.
